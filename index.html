The Missing Link — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The Missing Link is a communication (AAC) app that runs entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no analytics. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your communication boards, custom words, and tap history are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. We cannot see or recover them.

Internet

The app works fully offline and makes no network connections of its own.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app contains no ads, no chat with strangers, and no external links for children to follow.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
