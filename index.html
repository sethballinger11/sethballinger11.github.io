The Missing Link — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

The Missing Link is a communication (AAC) app made by Alien Atlas. It helps people who do not speak to communicate by tapping pictures and words, which the device reads aloud. This policy says what information the app collects, what it keeps on your device, and what leaves the device.

The short version

The Missing Link collects no personal information. There is no account to create, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. Alien Atlas runs no server for this app. Nothing is uploaded to us. Everything the app keeps stays on your device, except for the three cases listed under “What leaves your device”.

What the app stores on your device, and why

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this device, so it can work:

People and boards: the names, picture symbols, words and phrases set up for each person who communicates with the app.

Photos: if a caretaker adds a photo for a person, the photo is stored on this device as part of that person’s boards. It leaves the device only inside a backup file a caretaker chooses to export.

Communication history: a log of which tiles were tapped and when, so caretakers can review what was communicated. The log keeps at most 5,000 entries per person; older entries are removed automatically.

Your choices: settings such as the voice and the calming sounds, and your unlock preference — which caretaker unlock you chose to use first, and your backup.

Subscription: a small note that a subscription was confirmed, so the app can still open offline for up to seven days if Google Play cannot be reached.

Caretaker security

A caretaker PIN or pattern is stored only as a scrambled code. The app never stores the PIN or the pattern itself, and we cannot recover them. Fingerprint unlock: The app uses the fingerprint prompt built into Android. The app never sees or stores fingerprint information — your fingerprint stays in your device’s secure hardware, and Android only tells the app whether it matched. To be sure an unlock really came from that prompt, the app keeps a public key on the device — a code, not fingerprint information.

What leaves your device

Only three things ever leave this device. (1) Google Play: subscription checks and purchases. Google Play handles all payment details — we never see or store your card or bank information. (2) Your device’s speech engine: when a tile is tapped, its text goes to the speech engine on your device (for example, Google Speech Services), which speaks it aloud under its own privacy policy. The app does not record audio. (3) Backup files: only a file a caretaker chooses to export, and only to the place they choose to save or send it. Nothing else is transmitted. The app works fully offline for communication.

Backups you control

An exported backup file contains people’s names and their boards only (including any photos a caretaker added for people) — never the communication history, the PIN, unlock settings, or subscription information. Anyone who can open the file can read what is in it, so keep it somewhere safe. Restoring a backup replaces the device’s people and boards with the file’s and clears the device’s communication history; the app warns about this before it happens and keeps a copy so the restore can be undone.

Children and families

The Missing Link is made for communicators of any age, including children, and it is set up and managed by a caretaker. The communication area has no ads, no chat with strangers, and no links to outside content. Opening the caretaker area requires a double-tap and, once a caretaker PIN, unlock pattern, or fingerprint is set up, that check as well. Removing an icon or adding a word from the library asks for it every time. Restoring layouts and deleting icons there can be protected with an optional caretaker PIN, unlock pattern, or fingerprint.

Deleting your information

What the app stores stays on the device until you remove it or uninstall the app. Inside the app you can remove words from a board. A removed word is hidden, not erased, so it can be added back from the Library. People cannot be deleted inside the app at this time. To erase everything the app stored — boards, history, the PIN and settings — use Android Settings > Apps > The Missing Link > Storage > Clear storage. Uninstalling the app deletes the app’s stored information from the device. Uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel it in Google Play to stop being charged. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new date, and the copy inside the app will be updated with it.
